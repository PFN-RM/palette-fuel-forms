1 | About Palette Fuel Nutrition

Palette Fuel Nutrition provides applied sport and exercise nutrition support to athletes and physically active individuals.
Palette Fuel Nutrition is not a medical, dietetic, or clinical practice. Advice provided is within the scope of sport and exercise nutrition as defined by SENR and established evidence-based guidelines.

2 | Scope of Practice — Services Provided

  • ✓ Nutritional assessment and dietary analysis
  • ✓ Personalised sport and exercise nutrition plans and recommendations
  • ✓ Education on fuelling, hydration, recovery, and body composition for sport performance
  • ✓ Meal planning support and practical nutrition strategies
  • ✓ Monitoring and evaluation of nutrition habits and performance outcomes
  • ✓ Behaviour change support to improve nutrition practices

Services NOT Provided

  • ✗ Diagnosis, treatment or management of medical conditions
  • ✗ Clinical dietetic practice (requires HCPC registration)
  • ✗ Prescribing or recommending medication
  • ✗ Management of eating disorders or disordered eating (referral will be made)
  • ✗ Advice outside of the practitioner's current competency level

3-Day Food & Fluid Diary

Please record everything you eat and drink over 3 days — ideally 2 weekdays and 1 weekend day, including at least one training day. Complete it on the same day (don't try to remember the next day!)
